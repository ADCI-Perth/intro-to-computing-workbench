title: Learner Profiles
---

This course is for complete beginners, mostly working adults who want to move into cyber security, data or computing. These profiles are examples, not real people.

## Retail manager moving into cyber security

Daniel, 38, manages a hardware store and wants to start a Bachelor of Cyber Security. He uses computers every day but has never thought about what is inside them. He studies after his children are in bed, so the 6-hour weekly cap matters. Weeks 3 and 5 (permissions, logs, phishing and HTTPS) and the Essential Eight in Week 6 are where the course connects most clearly to the job he wants.

## Administration officer heading towards data science

Mei, 31, works in administration at a hospital and spends much of her day in spreadsheets. She wants to study data science. Week 2 (how data is really stored), Week 4 (algorithms and searching) and Week 6 (data quality, the data lifecycle and privacy) give her the foundations she will need, and Introduction to Programming builds on them with Python.

## Career changer on a locked-down work laptop

Aroha, 45, is a team leader in a government department. Her work laptop will not let her install anything, and some settings are blocked. Because every tool runs in the browser, and every task says what to do if a tool is blocked, she can study on her lunch break without asking IT for help.
