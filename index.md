**Introduction to Computing** is a six-week beginner course from the Australian Data and Cyber Institute (ADCI). It explains how computers work, how they store and move data, and how computing affects people and society. It prepares you for the computing units in a Bachelor of Cyber Security or a Bachelor of Data Science.

It is for **complete beginners**, especially working adults who want to move into cyber security, data or computing. You do not need any technical background. Each week takes **6 hours or less**.

**You do not need to install anything.** Everything runs in your web browser, so the course works on a locked-down work laptop. There is no programming language in this course. Python is taught in the separate Introduction to Programming course.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the six weeks you will be able to:

- **LO 1** Explain how a computer turns data into information and how its hardware parts work together, with a brief view of how computers got here.
- **LO 2** Represent data in binary and hexadecimal, explain how numbers, text, images and files are stored, and apply basic logic.
- **LO 3** Tell system software from application software, and use an operating system, including its command line, to manage files.
- **LO 4** Solve a problem with an algorithm, written as pseudocode and a flowchart, using sequence, selection and repetition.
- **LO 5** Explain how networks, the internet and the cloud work, and apply security, privacy and ethical principles to computing.

## How each week works

Each week is one episode in the menu. Open a week to see its parts: the week overview, the sections (such as 1.1 and 1.2), the Concepts Bridge and the practice task. Every week follows the same blocks:

| Block | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Watch | about 0.5 to 1 h | Short videos listed on the week overview |
| Read & practise | about 1 to 2 h | The week's sections: read, try each tool, do the exercises |
| Concepts Bridge | 30 min | One idea that links the week to cyber security or data science |
| Build | 2 h (3 h in Week 6) | Your portfolio item: a practice task with a worksheet |
| Quiz | 15 min | A short quiz in Canvas, marked automatically |

Your portfolio for each week is submitted at the end of the week and marked by your tutor.
