## Contributing

This is the Workbench (website) version of ADCI's *Introduction to Computing* course. The course content is maintained in the course folder, `intro-to-computing`, and converted into this lesson with `scripts/convert_from_course.py`.

### Making changes

- **Lesson content** (sections, week pages, Concepts Bridges, practice tasks and their files): change the course folder, then run the conversion again (see the README). Editing the episodes here directly will be overwritten by the next conversion.
- **Pages written only for this site** (`index.md`, `learners/setup.md`, `learners/reference.md`, `instructors/instructor-notes.md`, `profiles/learner-profiles.md`, `config.yaml`): edit them here.
- **Never add** model answers, quiz pools or other tutor-only files to this site. It is open to learners.

### Style

- Australian English, written for absolute beginners: short sentences and one idea per step.
- Browser only: learners install nothing.
- Each week must stay at 6 hours or less.
- Synthetic data only, and defensive security only.

### Reporting problems

Contact the course team.
