## About this course

- Six weeks, with a **hard cap of 6 hours a week** (32 h 45 m in total). Each week's overview has the time plan, which is the source of truth for timings. The `teaching` and `exercises` minutes on each episode are estimates based on it.
- For complete beginners, mostly working adults moving into cyber security, data or computing. Assume no technical background and only everyday arithmetic.
- **Browser only.** Learners install nothing, because many use locked-down work laptops. Do not add installed tools, even as optional extras.
- **No programming language and no SQL.** Algorithms are taught with pseudocode, flowcharts and the Little Man Computer. Data and databases are taught as ideas.
- Videos are third-party YouTube content: mainly the Crash Course Computer Science playlist (labelled **CC#n**), plus short videos from other channels (labelled **[Channel]**). Check every link before each intake.

## Assessment

- **Quizzes** run in Canvas: 6 questions drawn from a pool of 16 each week, marked automatically. The quiz pools are not on this site.
- **Portfolios** are the weekly practice tasks, marked by a tutor against four criteria (Accurate, Complete, Evidence and Explanation), each as **meets**, **partly** or **not yet**.
- **Model answers and marking notes are not on this site**, because it is open to learners. Tutors find them, with the full tutor guide, in the course folder: `weeks/week-N/practice/model_answer.md` and `curriculum/TUTOR_GUIDE.md`.
- Weightings, pass marks, deadlines and the Gen-AI declaration are still to be decided by ADCI.

## Teaching tips

- **Locked-down laptops:** each task names a fallback when a tool is blocked (for example, httpstatus.io when developer tools are blocked in Week 5). Learners are never marked down for IT restrictions.
- **JSLinux (Week 3)** is wiped when the page reloads, has no `man` pages (use `--help`), and drops characters when more than about 1,000 are pasted at once. The lab tells learners to upload the log file, or to paste it in two blocks.
- **Live values change:** DNS records, certificates and request counts in Week 5, and each learner's own hardware in Week 1, will differ from the notes. Mark the explanation, not exact matches.
- **Privacy:** learners must never record serial numbers, device names, user names, IP addresses, phone numbers or passwords. If a screenshot shows any of these, ask for a resubmission with it covered.
- **Invented businesses:** Harbour Street Physio appears in Weeks 3, 5 and 6, and Bean There café in Week 4. Keep their details consistent if you edit a task.
- **Security content is defensive only.**
